# Welcome

This month, we are celebrating the arrival of spring with three nights of sensory delights and slow intimate connection at the teahouse.

It is an invitation to step away from the everyday rhythm of the city and enter a softer, more intimate space. We will be offering gong-fu style tea service, beautiful soundscape, art making and cozy corners for connections and reflection.

Join us for an evening of rest, play and discovery. We can't wait to see you.
