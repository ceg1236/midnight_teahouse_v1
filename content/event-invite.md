# Welcome

This month, we are celebrating the arrival of spring with three nights of sensory delights and slow intimate connection at the teahouse.

It is an invitation to step away from the everyday rhythm of the city and enter a softer, more intimate space. We will be offering tea service, beautiful soundscape, art making and cozy corners for connections and reflection.

To keep our evenings intimate, the teahouse is open by reservation with limited seats. Please reserve a spot to gift a cozy evening to yourself or someone you love.

We look forward to sharing an evening with you.
