# Welcome
With spring in full bloom, we are tending to new growth at the teahouse—an upward and outward movement, from the soil toward the sun.

Join us for three evenings of tea salon, nestled in the creative parlors at the foot of Buena Vista Park.

Our teahouse is a space to slow down and settle in—an evening shaped by tea, live music, and the people in the room. Each night is unique, and you will find gongfu-style tea service, live music, art making, cozy corners for connection & reflection.

Come alone or with a friend—for rest, play, and discovery. We can’t wait to see you.
