# Welcome

As the nights grow long and cold, we invite you in from the dark for an evening of warmth, tea, and light. Join us for a holiday showcase — three evenings of gathering, music, and candlelight as the year draws to a close.

Our teahouse is a space to slow down and settle in — an evening shaped by tea, live music, and the people in the room. Each night is unique, and you will find gongfu-style tea service, live music, art making, cozy corners for connection & reflection.

This time, we're excited to present a selection of items we acquired on our recent trip to China and Taiwan — teas, wares, and various objects that piqued our interest. These items will be available for viewing and purchase — we hope you like them as much as we do!

Come alone or with a friend — for rest, play, and discovery. We can't wait to see you.
